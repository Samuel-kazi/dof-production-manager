Plan every show, episode and release in one place.

DOF Production Manager is the internal planning app of the Dawn of Faith Ministry media team. It keeps projects, recordings, deadlines and releases on one shared calendar.

Shared calendarMeetings, recording sessions, deadlines and releases for the whole team.
Shows & episodesTrack each episode from planning to published.
Printable schedulesDownload the day, week, month or year as a PDF.
Google Calendar invitesSend events to the team's Google Calendars and keep them up to date.

Who can use it

The app is used only by members of the Dawn of Faith media team, with accounts created by an administrator. It is not available to the public.

Google Calendar

An administrator can connect one Google account so the app can create, update and remove calendar invites for events planned in DOF Production Manager. Read how this data is handled in the privacy policy.
