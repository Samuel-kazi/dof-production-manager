Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

DOF Production Manager ("the app") is an internal planning tool run by Dawn of Faith Ministry for its media team. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information we keep

  • Team accounts: name, email address, role and an encrypted password, created by an administrator.
  • Planning data: projects, tasks, shows, episodes, releases and calendar events that team members enter.
  • Activity history: a record of important changes (who changed what and when).

This data is stored on servers controlled by Dawn of Faith Ministry. It is not sold, rented or used for advertising.

Google user data

An administrator may connect a Google account so the app can send Google Calendar invites. When connected, the app requests the calendar.events permission and your Google account's email address, and uses them only to:

  • create calendar events for DOF events that an administrator chooses to share, and invite the guests they pick;
  • update or remove those events when they change or are cancelled in the app;
  • read guests' replies (going, maybe, not going) for those events so planners can see who is attending.

The app does not read, change or delete any other events in your calendar. The Google refresh token is stored encrypted and is used only for the purposes above. Google user data is not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and deletion

An administrator can disconnect Google at any time in Settings → Google Calendar; the app then revokes its access and stops making changes. You can also remove access from your Google account permissions. Events already sent remain on guests' calendars, where they can be deleted like any other event. To ask for your account or data to be removed, contact us below.

Guests

People who receive an invite get Google's standard invitation email. Their email address and reply are kept with the event in the app so the team can plan.
